# Command: research

Usage: `/research [topic or question]`

Purpose: Combine wiki knowledge with external sources (when allowed) to investigate a topic and synthesize findings.

Procedure:
1. Check the wiki indices (`index/*.md`) for existing material on the topic and identify 5–10 candidate pages.
2. Read candidate wiki pages and extract relevant facts, claims, and citations.
3. If external research is allowed and needed, use browser tools to search for current authoritative information and verify findings across multiple sources.
4. Synthesize findings, combining wiki knowledge and external sources, with clear citations to both wiki pages and external URLs.
5. Optionally preserve valuable research as an analysis page (`wiki/analyses/[descriptive-title].md`) and update the index and log accordingly.

Notes: External browsing must follow the project's policies and user consents; always verify findings across multiple reputable sources.
